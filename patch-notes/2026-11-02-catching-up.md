# Patch Notes: Catching Up

Whoops, I haven't posted since the first one. Patch notes come with every update from here on, but here's roughly what's been added since:

**Gameplay**
- **Pass & play**: a whole game on one device, no account needed, works offline once installed
- **Round the table**: lay a tablet flat and everyone plays from their own side
- Install it to your home screen like an app
- Rebuilt phone layout with a new HUD and tap to roll
- The game pauses when everyone steps away
- Free camera around the table
- Your tab title flags when it's your turn

**Cosmetics**
- New slots: **kill effects**, **move trails**, and **emote packs** (Hampter, Cats, Capy, Groovin, and more)
- **Mythic sets**: full 6-piece themed sets you can only get from Golden Chests: Arcade, Voxel, Bombsite, Wild Encounter, All Aboard
- A lot more marbles, boards, dice, and nameplates, 200+ items total
- Chests give you things you don't own first. Dupes only start once you've finished a rarity
- **Tags**: pick a main tag and shorthands in the Locker
- New Unobtainable tab in the Locker

**Luckiest**
- Monthly race: best six rate in a single game wins the month and picks a piece of the exclusive **Luckiest** set
- The leaderboard shows who's on track to win this month

**Social**
- Lobby and post-game chat
- Emotes at the table, spectators included
- Rematch voting and host handoffs
- Discord avatars on nameplates

**Packs** (optional, cosmetic only)
- **Supporter Pack**: badge plus exclusive cosmetics
- **Painter Pack**: while you're seated, everyone at the table can pick any custom colour
- The Halloween Pack ran through October

**Fixes & Performance**
- Smoother frame rate, no more mid-game shader hitches, lighter on phones
- Discord Activity lobbies survive server restarts
- Idle lobbies and tables close on their own
