# Patch Notes: Wellspring

**Cosmetics**
- New mythic set in the Golden Chest: **Wellspring**, based on my other game [Wellspring](https://ryanpolasky.com/wellspring)
- **Freefall** marble: your color in bricks, scrolling past like you're mid-drop
- **Jump In** board: a whole well to fall down, plus a little minion keeping watch outside it
- **Gem Count** dice: gem pips that shimmer
- **Jump In** nameplate: your name in the real Wellspring pixel font
- **Stomp** kill effect: land on their head, they pop into gems and a soul, you bounce off
- **Magic Missile** trail rounds out the set
