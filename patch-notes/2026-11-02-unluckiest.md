# Patch Notes: Unluckiest
Luckiest finally has an evil twin.

**Unluckiest**
- New monthly title: **Unluckiest** goes to the worst single-game six rate of the month (30+ of your own rolls to qualify)
- Hold it at month's end to pick a piece of the new **Leaky Roof** set. Once you own the whole set, it pays out coins instead
- Find it in its own **Leaderboard** tab and under Luckiest on your profile

**Cosmetics**
- **Duct Taped** marble: cracked, taped, still leaking
- **Water Damage** board: stained, warped, and actively dripping into a bucket
- **Half Full** dice: a clear die with water sloshing inside
- **Puddle** nameplate, **Bucket** kill effect and **Leak** trail round out the set
